Source, Prediction, Assignment

These labels answer different questions. Source is where the selected motif or structural model came from; prediction category is how the final motif is presented to users; assignment type explains whether the TF-to-motif link is direct, homologous, or structure-predicted. For ModCRE predictions, structural source is shown separately as Homology model or AlphaFold3 model.

Practical interpretation

QuestionRecommended answer
Which motif should I use first?Prefer Known motifs, then Nearest Neighbor (>70%), then Nearest Neighbor (50-70%), then ModCRE predictions.
What does the ModCRE source mean?The prediction is ModCRE. Its structural source is reported separately as Homology model or AlphaFold3 model.
Are predicted motifs low quality?No. ModCRE motifs are structure-derived predictions and should be inspected with their region, template, and model support.
Why do some motifs have identity percentages?The percentage describes similarity to the TF used to transfer the PWM. Known motifs do not need a transfer percentage.
Why are some referenced motifs missing?Some archive records do not contain a generated PWM and are labeled Missing MEME in public views.
